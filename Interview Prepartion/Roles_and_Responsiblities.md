## Functional Direction & Abbreviations

**Functional Direction:**

- **Bug Fixes:** Across Dev/QA/Production  
- **Complexity Levels:** Straightforward, Difficult, Complex, Significantly Complex  
- **Process Improvement:** From code to delivery  
- **Code Quality:** Operational excellence  
- **Self-Learn:** Initiative-based domain learning  

**Abbreviations:**

- **Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced / Specialized** – Levels of technical knowledge  
- **Broader Tech Community:** Open source and global leaders engagement
